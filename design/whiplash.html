Whiplash – Opening Credits

Whiplash, a 2014 film by award winning director Damien Chazelle, is an electrifying exploration of obsession, ambition, and the fine line between greatness and destruction. Set in a top-tier music school, it follows a young drummer who’ll do anything to impress his ruthless instructor. This film is all about ambition and how far people will go for perfection. It serves as a reminder of how powerful art can be when passion takes over. The opening credits for Whiplash set the pulse for the film that follows. Creating a visual language that mirrors the film’s rhythm and music.
