Chesapeake Bay Program

The Chesapeake Bay Field Guide is a thoughtfully designed digital resource created to help visitors explore and learn about the diverse wildlife, natural habitats, and rich history of the Chesapeake Bay region. The website brings the bay’s unique ecosystem to life through an engaging and easy-to-navigate experience, allowing users to discover the many species of critters that inhabit the region, explore their habitats, and learn about the environmental and historical significance of the area. The website features dedicated pages for different critter types and habitats, as well as what to look for, creating an informative and interactive experience for nature enthusiasts, students, and curious visitors. Through organized content, intuitive navigation, and engaging visual design, the Chesapeake Bay Field Guide aims to make learning about the region accessible and enjoyable while encouraging a deeper appreciation for the wildlife, ecosystems, and history that make the Chesapeake Bay so special.
