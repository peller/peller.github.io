Center for Faculty Excellence

The Center for Faculty Excellence at American University cultivates opportunities for professional growth and innovation in teaching, scholarship, service, career advancement, and leadership for AU faculty. In 2025, the Center for Faculty Excellence (previously the Center for Teaching, Research, and Learning) was launched as a reimagined center dedicated to supporting faculty at all stages of their careers, across teaching, research, service, and professional growth. The newly reimagined center required an updated logo, to be utilized on the website, large-scale signage, event posters, nametags, business cards, etc.
