in a pickle

in a pickle is a pickleball league designed to bring people together through friendly competition and community. The league aims to make pickleball approachable and exciting for players of all skill levels. From its custom paddles to its engaging promotional materials, in a pickle creates a memorable experience both on and off the court. The branding includes a unique winter-themed paddle pattern encouraging players to join the league during the colder months (indoor in cooler locations). In a Pickle aims to build a welcoming community where players can stay active, connect with others, and enjoy the sport all year round.
